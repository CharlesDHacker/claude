# Prestige and Progression

## Prestige: Tidal Reset
- Late-game option to reset coins, subs and tanks in exchange for **Pearls**.
- Pearls give a permanent income multiplier and unlock exclusive cosmetics.
- **Keeps:** the collection log and cosmetics, so resets never erase bragging rights.
- Each Tidal Reset raises the bar: previously cheap tiers still gate zones, but you move through them faster.

## Medium-term goals
- **Collection log:** per-zone, with a reward for completing each, so players do not just rush the deepest area.
- **Achievements:** first Legendary, first golden mutation, first full tank set.
- **Daily rewards and quests:** a small daily dive goal (catch 3 creatures in the reef) to drive return visits.

## Long-term goals
- Event zones with limited creatures.
- Leaderboards for most valuable base, rarest collection.
- Guilds (later): shared protection, group goals.

## Pacing targets (tune in playtests)
- First 10 minutes: Scuba Starter bought, first creatures placed.
- First hour: Scuba Pro and first sub.
- First week: Thermal Sub and first prestige.
