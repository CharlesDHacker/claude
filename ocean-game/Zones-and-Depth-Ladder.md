# Zones and Depth Ladder

Zones are the main content lever: each brings its own creatures, hazard and sub requirement.

## Starter ladder (MVP candidates)
| Zone | Gate | Creatures | Hazard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shallows / Coral reef | Scuba gear | Clownfish, turtles | Low |
| Kelp forest / Shipwrecks | Better tank | Sharks, rays | Light predators |
| Twilight zone | Basic sub | Anglerfish, jellies | Darkness, pressure |
| Volcanic vents | Heat-rated sub | Tube worms, lava eels | Heat damage |
| Mariana trench | Deep-sea sub | Giant squid, abyssal creatures | Extreme pressure |
| Event / Prehistoric | Time-limited or late game | Megalodon, plesiosaurs | Boss-style |

## Natural ocean zones
- Sandy flats / seagrass meadows: starter, rays, seahorses, manatees.
- Mangrove roots: murky, crabs, mudskippers; visibility is the hazard.
- Kelp forest: tangled vegetation slows you; otters and sharks.
- Shipwreck graveyard: loot crates plus eels in the hulls.
- Underwater caves / blue holes: limited oxygen, albino cave creatures.
- Open-ocean pelagic: no landmarks, fast predators (tuna, marlin), whales.
- Hydrothermal vents: heat damage, tube worms, ghostly shrimp.
- Whale fall: skeleton on the floor, scavengers; a rare event spot.
- Abyssal plains: vast, dark, bioluminescent creatures.
- Mariana trench: final natural zone, pressure is the hazard.

## Cold-water zones
- Arctic ice shelf: cold damage, seals, narwhals, ice to dive under.
- Antarctic depths: giant isopods, colossal squid.
- Frozen cavern: crystal formations, rare "ice" mutation.

## Fantasy and sci-fi
- Atlantis ruins: ancient architecture, relics, mythical creatures.
- Sunken pirate cove: ghost ships, cursed treasure, skeletal variants.
- Kraken's lair: late-game boss zone.
- Crystal caverns: glowing minerals, gem-like creatures.
- Alien trench: space-themed crash site, otherworldly creatures.
- Mythical coral garden: rainbow reefs, mermaids, sea dragons.

## Prehistoric
- Cretaceous seas: mosasaurs, plesiosaurs.
- Cambrian explosion: trilobites, Anomalocaris.
- Megalodon territory: high-risk, high-reward hunt.

## Seasonal and event (limited-time)
- Winter holiday reef: festive fish, ornament-like creatures.
- Halloween graveyard: ghost fish, pumpkin pufferfish.
- Summer luau lagoon: tropical creatures, beach party items.
- Lunar, Valentine, Easter and other real-world events.

## Zone ideas
- Zone-specific hazards: open-ocean currents, cracking Arctic ice, timed vent eruptions.
- Hidden sub-areas needing a specific upgrade.
- Zone bosses and per-zone collection logs.

## Release rhythm
Launch with 3-4 core zones, then one new zone or event every 2-3 weeks. Fantasy and seasonal zones are the update headlines; natural zones are the foundation.
