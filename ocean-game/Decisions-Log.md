# Decisions Log

Calls made on the user's behalf ("you're the brains"). Veto any of them.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stealing: base raids first**, sub piracy added after launch | Simpler to build and test; piracy layers on later |
| 2 | **Tone: colorful and playful, all ages, mild spooky only in the deepest zones** | Widest Roblox audience; horror can come as event content |
| 3 | **Working title: Reef Raiders** (fallback: Tide Thieves) | Says diving and stealing in two words; revisit if search is crowded |
| 4 | **Solo-dev scope assumed** | Favors content that scales (zones, creatures) over authored set pieces |
| 5 | **MVP zones: Coral Reef and Twilight Zone** | A clear contrast: bright and safe versus dark and risky |
| 6 | **No Robux sales of sub tiers 3-7** | Keeps progression meaningful |
