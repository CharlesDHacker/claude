# Base and Tank Design

## The beach
Shared server beach with one plot per player (12-16 per server). Plots sit along the shore so players can see and visit each other. The ocean entrance (dock) is at the plot's seaward edge.

## Tanks
- Start with 2-3 tank slots; upgrade to 12.
- **Tank sizes:** Small (fish), Medium (turtles, rays), Large (sharks, squid), Giant (whales, Mythics). A creature only fits a matching or larger tank, which is a reason to keep expanding.
- Each tank earns income from its creature and shows it swimming inside, so your base is also your trophy display.

## Income collection
- Income accumulates in each tank. Players press collect (or walk over a collect pad) to bank it.
- **Offline earnings** accumulate for up to about 2 hours (longer with upgrades), so returning feels rewarding without punishing short sessions.

## Upgrades
- More tanks, bigger tanks, auto-collect, locks, guard creatures.
- **Cosmetics:** decorations, tank themes, dock skins, sub skins.

## Visiting and social
- Anyone can visit and look; only raids can take.
- Showcase wall for rare finds and the collection log, which creates bragging rights and clips.

## Open questions
- Should creatures breed or fuse? (Parked: adds depth but also scope.)
- Should plots persist visually across servers (yes via saved layout) while creatures are the only thing that moves in raids?
