# First Two Zones (MVP)

## Zone 1: Coral Reef (0-30 m, Scuba Starter)
**Feel:** bright, colorful, safe. Teaches the dive loop.
- **Hazard:** none except oxygen. No damage.
- **Landmarks:** a sunken anchor, a coral arch, a school of fish to follow.
- **Spawns** (about 6 creatures):
  - Common: clownfish, damselfish
  - Uncommon: sea turtle
  - Rare: manta ray
  - Epic: reef shark (mild, slow to flee)
  - Legendary: seahorse king (rare, tiny, hard to find)
- **Mutation odds:** golden 1 in 50; glowing 1 in 200.
- **Catch mechanic:** swim up, press interact, a quick timing bar; shiny creatures need a harder bar.
- **Goal:** fill the cargo (3 slots), return, earn the first 500 for Scuba Pro.

## Zone 2: Twilight Zone (100-300 m, Explorer Sub)
**Feel:** dark, eerie, bioluminescent; the first sense of risk.
- **Hazards:** darkness (headlights needed), pressure bar (hull stress at max depth), roaming anglerfish that dim your lights if it hits you.
- **Landmarks:** glowing jellyfish fields, a sunken research station, a whale carcass.
- **Spawns** (about 6 creatures):
  - Common: lanternfish
  - Uncommon: glowing jellyfish
  - Rare: vampire squid
  - Epic: anglerfish (also the hazard)
  - Legendary: giant squid
  - Mythic: ghost whale (event-spawn only, once per server per hour)
- **Mutation odds:** glowing 1 in 100; albino 1 in 400.
- **Catch mechanic:** same bar, plus a stealth element: loud boosting spooks creatures, quiet drifting keeps them close.
- **Goal:** haul 10 creatures per trip, afford the Thermal Sub.

## Why these two for the MVP
They contrast safe/bright with dark/risky, test both dive types (scuba and sub), and give two clear rarity ladders without needing boss or event systems.
