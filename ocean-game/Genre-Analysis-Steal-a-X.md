# Genre Analysis: "Steal a ___"

From memory (verify against what is currently on the platform; I am not certain about a game called "Steal an Egg" specifically).

## Core loop of the genre
1. Get items (shop, conveyor belt, eggs) in rarity tiers.
2. Place them in your base; they earn currency passively.
3. Steal from other players' bases.
4. Defend with locks, timed doors, traps; locks expire so players keep returning.
5. Reinvest in rarer items, then rebirth/prestige.

## Why it works
- Passive income gives players a reason to come back.
- Stealing creates drama and clip-worthy moments for streamers and YouTube.
- Rarity and luck mechanics (eggs, mutations, events) give "I got a rare one" excitement.
- Easy to learn: buy, place, defend, steal.
- Monetizes easily: luck boosts, speed boosts, extra slots, lock upgrades.

## Weaknesses (our opening)
- Many near-identical reskins; an original twist stands out.
- Being robbed feels unfair to newcomers; add protection or recovery.
- Griefing and exploiting: needs server-side validation.

## Ways to differentiate
- A skill element in the heist (puzzle, parkour route, co-op steal).
- A distinct theme or hook (this game: playable ocean diving with depth gates).
- Social structure: guilds protecting each other's bases, trading so loss is not purely negative.
- Cozy or horror framing instead of the usual chaotic meme art.
