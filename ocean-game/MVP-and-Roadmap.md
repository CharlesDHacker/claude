# MVP and Roadmap

## MVP (smallest version worth playtesting)
- One beach base per player, 2-3 tank slots.
- Two zones (reef + twilight) and one sub upgrade.
- About 12 creatures across 4 rarity tiers.
- Passive income.
- A simple lock and steal mechanic.

Test question: is the reef-to-twilight dive fun, and does the steal feel fair? If yes, the rest is mostly content.

## After MVP
1. Trench and volcanic zones, more sub tiers.
2. Mutations, collection log, newcomer protection.
3. First event zone (seasonal or prehistoric).
4. One new zone or event every 2-3 weeks.
