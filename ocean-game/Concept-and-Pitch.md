# Concept and Pitch

**Pitch:** Everyone's base sits on the same beach. Beyond it is the open sea. You scuba dive into zones (coral reef, twilight zone, Mariana trench, volcanic vents and more), catch creatures, and bring them home to earn passive income. You cannot reach the deepest zones right away: you need to buy better gear and submarines. Other players can steal from your base, so you defend it.

**Why it could work**
- Depth is a natural progression gate, so unlocking zones feels earned.
- The ocean is very expandable: prehistoric life, holiday fish, fantasy and sci-fi zones.
- Playable diving (oxygen, darkness, pressure, predators) adds skill to a genre that is often menu-only.
- Return trips with a full cargo hold create real risk.

**Reference genre:** the "Steal a ___" trend led by Steal a Brainrot. See [Genre Analysis](Genre-Analysis-Steal-a-X.md).
