# Core Loop and Systems

## Loop
1. **Dive** from the beach into a zone; catch or collect creatures.
2. **Return** to your beach base; place creatures in tanks or pens that earn passive income.
3. **Upgrade** gear and sub to reach deeper zones with rarer creatures.
4. **Defend** your base against other players.
5. **Prestige** for permanent bonuses.

## Submarine tiers
Each sub tier is a main purchase. Stats: depth rating, oxygen/battery, cargo capacity, speed, heat resistance, light. The sub unlocks access, not instant income, so it feels fair rather than pay-to-win.

## Stealing: two options (could do both)
- **Beach raids:** sneak into bases while owners are diving. Owners buy locks, alarms, guard creatures.
- **Ocean piracy:** ram or hijack a sub on the way back with a full cargo hold. Adds risk to every dive.

## Other systems
- **Rarity and mutations:** glowing, golden, albino variants at low odds.
- **Oxygen and pressure:** a timer that keeps dives short and pushes players back to the surface.
- **Newcomer protection:** a grace period before new players can be robbed.
- **Events:** time-limited zones and creatures as the content engine.
- **Collection log:** a log per zone with completion rewards, so players do not only rush the deepest area.
- **Day/night cycle, weather and tides:** some creatures only at night; storms raise rare spawns but make the surface dangerous.
- **Hidden sub-areas** inside zones that need a specific upgrade.
- **Zone bosses:** roaming predators or co-op giant-creature hunts.
