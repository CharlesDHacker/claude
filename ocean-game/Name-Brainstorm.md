# Name Brainstorm

## Candidates
- Genre-style: Steal a Sea Creature, Steal a Fish, Steal a Shark, Dive and Steal.
- Ocean/depth: Deep Dive Tycoon, Abyss Hunters, Dive Deeper, Down Below, Fathom, The Deep End.
- Playful: Catch & Keep, Reef Raiders, Tide Thieves, Plunder Cove, Sunken Loot, Fin Thieves.
- Collection: Aquarium Heist, Pearl Diver, Deep Sea Collectors, Abyss Aquarium.

## Shortlist
1. **Reef Raiders:** easy to say, hints at diving and stealing, colorful tone.
2. **Fathom:** clean, depth built in; suits an atmospheric tone.
3. **Tide Thieves:** playful, signals the steal mechanic.
4. **Abyss Aquarium:** collecting plus deep zones; ages well as content expands.

## Checks before committing
- Search Roblox for the name (taken or buried by similar games?).
- Say it out loud: kids share games by voice.
- Easy to spell from a thumbnail or YouTube title.
- Holds up as content expands: "Reef" alone may feel limiting once prehistoric and space zones arrive.

Tone decides it (cozy and colorful, spooky and deep, or chaotic and funny): undecided.
