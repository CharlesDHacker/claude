# Seasons and Events

The mountain rotates through four seasons on a server-time schedule. Same map, reskinned, with a different egg pool and a few rule tweaks, which keeps the content load manageable for a solo dev.

## Rotation
- **2 weeks per season**, an 8-week cycle: Winter, Spring, Summer, Autumn.
- The current season is shown in the village and on the lift sign, with a countdown to the next one.
- Holiday events overlay a season (for example a Christmas week in Winter, a haunted lodge week in Autumn).

## The four seasons
| Season | Look | Egg pool | Terrain and gear twist |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Winter** (base) | Deep snow, fireplaces, lights | Foxes, penguins, yetis, snow leopards | Skis and sleds as normal |
| **Spring** | Melt, streams, flowers, mud | Chicks, bunnies, frogs, lambs | Streams slow you; snow only on the upper peaks; **mud boards** replace skis low down |
| **Summer** | Green alpine meadows, grass slopes | Marmots, goats, butterflies, eagles | **Grass sledding** and **mountain boards**; no ice hazards, more wildlife |
| **Autumn** | Orange leaves, fog, harvest | Owls, bats, hedgehogs, pumpkin critters | Fog reduces visibility; leaf piles hide raiders and eggs |

## Rules for seasonal content
- **Seasonal pets** only hatch from that season's eggs, with an aurora/golden-style mutation chance of their own.
- **Pets return:** each seasonal pet comes back when its season rotates around, so nobody is locked out forever. Event-only mythics can be rarer and return less often.
- **Collection log** shows all seasonal pets, with the missing ones marked "returns in Summer", which creates anticipation instead of regret.
- **Core gameplay does not change**: incubators, warmth, raids and NPCs work in all seasons. Warmth is still needed in spring and summer (reskinned as heat lamps, sun rocks, or cooling shade for some eggs).

## Event ideas
Holiday village, haunted lodge, aurora peak, spring egg hunt, summer festival, anniversary mythic.
