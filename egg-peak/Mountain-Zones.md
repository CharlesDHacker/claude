# Mountain Zones

Starting values; tune in playtests.

| # | Zone | Gate | Egg theme | Hazard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Base village / bunny slope | None | Basic eggs (rabbit, chick) | None |
| 1 | Pine forest | Winter boots | Foxes, owls, deer | Low; trees slow you |
| 2 | Frozen lake | Ice skates | Penguins, seals | Thin ice cracks |
| 3 | Ice caves | Headlamp + rope | Bats, crystal critters | Darkness, falling icicles |
| 4 | Summit lodge | Gondola pass | Yetis, snow leopards | Wind pushes you |
| 5 | Blizzard peak | Heated gear | Mythic eggs | Cold meter drains health |
| E | Event zones | Limited-time | Holiday, aurora, ancient glacier | Varies |

## Travel
- **Lift or gondola** up (waiting is a social moment, and a chance to be ambushed).
- **Ski or sled** down fast; also the escape route when carrying a stolen egg.
- Each zone has 4-6 egg spawn points and a shop egg stand.

## Event zone ideas
Holiday village, aurora peak, ancient glacier (prehistoric pets), spring melt (easter-style eggs), Halloween haunted lodge.

## Release rhythm
Launch with zones 0-3, add the summit and blizzard peak within a month, then an event zone every 2-3 weeks.
