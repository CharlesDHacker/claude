# Concept and Pitch

**Pitch:** Everyone's ski lodge sits at the foot of one big mountain. Ride up to different slopes, find and buy eggs, haul them back to your lodge to incubate, and hatch pets that earn passive income. Other players can sneak into your lodge and steal your eggs, then try to escape down the mountain before you catch them.

**Core loop**
1. Ride up the mountain (lift, sled, or ski down).
2. Find or buy eggs and carry them back to the lodge.
3. Incubate them; hatching takes time and rolls a rarity and mutation.
4. Hatched pets earn income while they live in the lodge.
5. Defend your lodge, raid others.
6. Upgrade lodge, gear and zones; prestige.

**What makes it different from other "Steal a ___" games**
- Cozy winter setting instead of meme chaos.
- The escape is playable: stolen eggs get carried **downhill** on skis or sleds, with the owner chasing.
- Eggs need warmth, so lodge layout and fireplaces matter and raiders can sabotage heat.
- The mountain is the progression spine; higher zones need better gear.
- Seasons and holidays are a natural fit.
