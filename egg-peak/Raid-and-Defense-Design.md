# Raid and Defense Design

Starting values; all checks server-side.

## Raiding
- A raider enters an unlocked lodge, holds interact on an incubator for 2-3 seconds (visible progress bar, owner gets an alarm), and takes **the egg** from it.
- Only **eggs** can be stolen, never hatched pets.
- While carrying a stolen egg the raider is slowed on foot and glows, so the owner can spot them. Skis and sleds are the escape: faster, but harder to steer with the egg.
- The raider must reach their own lodge to bank the egg.

## The chase
- The owner can chase on skis or a sled; catching the raider (tag) returns the egg to the incubator.
- Terrain matters: shortcuts, jumps, trees, so the chase is skill, not just speed.
- Raiders can drop decoy eggs or use items to slow pursuers (snowballs, ice patches).

## Defenses
- **Door locks** (timed, upgradeable).
- **Snow traps** slow intruders.
- **Guard dogs** (St. Bernards) chase and tag.
- **Avalanche button** buries the lodge entrance briefly.
- **Warmth sabotage** works both ways: raiders douse fires, owners relight.

## NPC pressure
NPC skiers chase egg carriers by zone and join the chase on top of the owner. See [NPC Chasers](NPC-Chasers.md).

## Anti-grief
- First 20 minutes of play are raid-proof.
- A lodge cannot lose more than one egg per raid, and a victim can only be raided a few times per 10 minutes.
- Legendary and Mythic eggs are protected for a few minutes after they are found.
- Insurance: the owner gets a share of the egg's value back as consolation currency.
