# Eggs and Incubation

Starting values; tune in playtests.

## Egg tiers
| Tier | Hatch time | Pet income/s | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 1 min | 1 | Base village, bunny slope |
| Uncommon | 5 min | 5 | Pine forest |
| Rare | 20 min | 25 | Frozen lake |
| Epic | 1 hr | 150 | Ice caves |
| Legendary | 4 hr | 1,000 | Summit |
| Mythic | 12 hr | 7,500 | Blizzard peak / events |

Each egg rolls its pet when it hatches, not when found, so every hatch is a small suspense moment.

## Incubators
- Start with 2 incubator slots, up to 12 in the late game.
- Eggs only progress while **warm** and inside the lodge.
- Eggs found out on the mountain are vulnerable until you bring them home.

## Warmth mechanic
- Incubators need heat from a fireplace, stove or heater placed nearby.
- Warmth radius makes layout matter: put the high-value incubators by the fire.
- Raiders can **douse the fire** (takes a few seconds) to slow hatching in a lodge they are robbing.
- Heater upgrades widen the radius and speed hatching slightly.

## Mutations on hatch
Frost (x2 income), golden (x3), aurora (x5), at low odds. Aurora comes from event zones only.

## Pets
- Live in the lodge in pens or on perches; each earns income per second.
- Pet size matches pen size (small, medium, large, giant), which gives a reason to expand.
- Pets cannot be stolen once hatched; **only eggs** are raid targets (see raids). This keeps the "steal an egg" fantasy clean and protects hatched pets.
