# Lodge and Base Design

## Layout
A shared village at the mountain base, one lodge plot per player (12-16 per server). The lodge door faces the village square; a ski ramp behind each lodge leads to the mountain.

## Inside the lodge
- Incubator slots with fireplace, stove or heater.
- Pet pens for hatched pets, visible and cute.
- A **trophy wall** for rare hatches and the collection log.
- A cozy common area (cosmetic: couches, rugs, cocoa bar) that is purely decorative and social.

## Income
Hatched pets earn income; coins accumulate in a jar and are collected with one press (or auto-collect upgrade). Offline earnings cap at about 2 hours, longer with upgrades.

## Upgrades
- More incubators, bigger pens, better heaters.
- **Lodge tiers:** Cabin, Chalet, Lodge, Grand Lodge, each with bigger interior and more slots.
- Cosmetics: decor, exterior skins, skis and sleds.

## Social
Anyone can visit and admire; only raids take. Visiting players can emote and leave "cheers" on the trophy wall.
