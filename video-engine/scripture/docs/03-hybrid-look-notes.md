# Hybrid 2D/3D look: target notes (from reference videos, Nov 2026)

Direction: drop the ancient ukiyo-e styling. Rich, high-quality hybrid: real 3D sets (three.js, prototype in proto/) with 2D or cel-shaded characters that blend in.

## Backgrounds must be FULL
- Ground never flat: dirt paths, rocks, roots, grass tufts, leaves, ferns, puddles, petals, scattered props (barrels, crates, signs, lanterns).
- Depth layers: big foreground framing elements close to the lens (trunks, vines, leaves, gate posts), detailed midground, soft blurred background (depth of field).
- Hero set pieces that fill the frame: giant trees, ships, icebergs, castles, armies to the horizon.
- Light drives every shot: god-rays through canopies, dappled light/shadow patches, rim light, glowing magic, coloured fog, warm/cool contrast.
- Each location has its own colour script (jungle green+gold, ice blue, sakura pink+amber, neon purple for power, crimson for villains).

## Characters blend in
- Characters cast real shadows on the 3D ground and pick up scene light (rim + tint); consider cel-shaded 3D models for hosts and cast.

## Edit
- Average shot ~1.3-2 s on action, holds on emotional beats; constant camera motion (dolly, orbit, whip, slam-stop).
- Centred punch captions (2-3 words, yellow/red keywords), giant impact words, explainer graphics (meters, icons).
- Bloom, DOF, motion blur, per-scene grade.

## First episode after the reset
Kiyohime: frame-story script (hosts inside the story world, callback ending), story characters with their own voices, quieter music bed (~-19 dB under voice).
