# Episode scripts

Format: **WHO:** line. `POP` = giant gold pop word. *Italics* = picture. Selah narrates every flashback line unless marked otherwise.
Each script maps to the structure of one of the reference videos.

## EP03 · THE BRONZE SERPENT, *one look, and you live*
Numbers 21:4–9; John 3:14–15; 2 Kings 18:4 · reference shape: Medusa (one look...)

*Night desert. Toby frozen, staring at the sand by his feet.*
**TOBY:** SELAH! SNAKE!
**SELAH:** Don't look at the snake. Look up.
**TOBY:** POP **LOOK UP?!**
**SELAH:** Once, a whole nation was told the same thing.
*Title: THE BRONZE SERPENT, one look, and you live*
Israel was walking through the desert... | and they started to complain. | About the water. About the food. | About the bread God sent them from the sky.
*Snakes slither out of the rocks.* Then the snakes came. | Fiery serpents. | People were bitten... and many died.
**TOBY:** Did God take the snakes away?!
**SELAH:** No. | *Moses praying.* The people begged Moses to pray. | And God told him to make... another snake.
**TOBY:** POP **ANOTHER SNAKE?!**
A snake made of bronze. | Lifted high on a pole. | *Glint of gold over the camp.* And anyone who was bitten... | if they looked at it... | they lived.
**TOBY:** Just... looking?
**SELAH:** Just looking.
*Centuries later, a night conversation. Nicodemus and Jesus by lamplight.* Hundreds of years later, a teacher came to Jesus at night. | And Jesus said:
**JESUS (off-screen voice, warm):** "As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness... so must the Son of Man be lifted up."
*The pole's shadow on a hill becomes the shape of a cross. Gold.* Lifted up. | On a cross.
**SELAH:** The snakes were still there. | But so was the cure.
*Back in the present.*
**TOBY:** *(slowly looks up at the stars)* ...So we look up.
**SELAH:** We look up.
**TOBY:** *(glances down at the "snake")* ...Selah. It's a stick.
**SELAH:** I know.
Post caption footnote: Israel later started worshipping the bronze snake itself, so King Hezekiah smashed it (2 Kings 18:4). Look *through* it, not *at* it.

## EP04 · THE WELL OF BETHLEHEM, *the water David poured away*
2 Samuel 23:13–17 (1 Chronicles 11:15–19) · reference shape: Alexander (direct parallel)

*A cave. Firelight. David holds a dripping helmet and slowly tips it.*
**TOBY:** Whyyyy is he POURING it out?!
**SELAH:** That's King David.
**TOBY:** But it's the ONLY water!
**SELAH:** Watch what it does to his men.
*Title: DAVID, the water he poured away*
David was hiding in a cave. | The Philistine army held his hometown. | Bethlehem.
*David, tired, looking out at the valley.* And David said, almost to himself:
**DAVID:** "Oh... if only someone could get me a drink from the well of Bethlehem, by the gate."
Three of his mighty men heard him. | *Three silhouettes run at night.* That night, they broke through the whole Philistine army... | drew water from that well... | and carried it back.
**TOBY:** POP **FOR A DRINK?!**
*David holds the helmet. Firelight on the water.* David looked at the water... | and he wouldn't drink it.
**DAVID:** "This is the blood of the men who risked their lives."
*He pours it out. Slow motion. The water catches the light.* So he poured it out... | to the Lord.
**TOBY:** He WASTED it!
**SELAH:** No. | He gave God the most expensive thing he had.
*The three mighty men watching, wet-eyed, standing taller.* And his men knew... | their king thought their lives were worth more than his thirst.
*Present. Toby holds his waterskin. He pours a tiny trickle onto the sand, very solemnly.*
**SELAH:** ...That's not how it works.
**TOBY:** ...Can I drink the rest?

## EP05 · SAMSON'S RIDDLE, *out of the strong came something sweet*
Judges 13:4–5, 14:5–18; Numbers 6:6 · reference shape: Sphinx (interactive riddle with a 3·2·1 countdown)

*Vineyards of Timnah. A lion's skeleton in the grass, bees humming.*
**SELAH:** Toby, don't touch that.
**TOBY:** It's just bones!
**SELAH:** That's what Samson thought.
*Title: SAMSON, the riddle that trapped him*
At his own wedding feast, Samson gave thirty guests a riddle:
**SAMSON (giant, cocky):** "Out of the eater came something to eat. | Out of the strong came something sweet."
Seven days to answer. | Or they'd owe him thirty sets of clothes.
**SELAH:** Can YOU solve it? | *3... 2... 1...* (gold numbers, like the reference)
**TOBY:** POP **WHAT'S THE ANSWER?!**
*Flashback inside the flashback.* On the road to Timnah, a lion attacked him. | God's Spirit came on him... | and he tore it apart with his bare hands.
Later, bees had built a hive in its body. | So he scooped out the honey... | and ate it.
**TOBY:** From a DEAD LION?!
**SELAH:** And here's the secret. | Samson was a Nazirite, set apart to God from birth... | and Nazirites must never touch anything dead.
*He gives honey to his parents.* He even gave some to his parents... | and never told them where it came from.
*The guests threaten his bride. She weeps for seven days.* The guests couldn't solve it. | So they threatened his bride. | She cried until he told her. | And she told them.
**GUESTS (smug):** "What is sweeter than honey? What is stronger than a lion?"
*Samson's face falls.*
**SELAH:** The strongest man in the world... | trapped by his own secret.
*Present.*
**TOBY:** *(hiding something behind his back)* ...
**SELAH:** What's behind your back?
**TOBY:** ...Honey.

## EP06 · THE ROOSTER, *the sound that made a grown man cry*
Luke 22:31–34, 54–62; John 18:15–27; John 21:9–19 · reference shape: Eos / dew (a sound or object hiding a sorrow), Siren (grief → wings)

*Dawn over a village. A rooster crows right next to Toby's ear.*
**TOBY:** UGH! ROOSTERS!
**SELAH:** That sound once made a grown man cry.
**TOBY:** POP **A ROOSTER?!**
*Title: PETER, the rooster's crow*
Peter was Jesus's best friend. | He said: "Lord, I'd die for you."
**JESUS (gentle):** "Peter... before the rooster crows today, you will deny me three times."
*Night. A courtyard. Soldiers warming hands at a charcoal fire.* That night, Jesus was arrested. | Peter followed... | and stood by a charcoal fire to keep warm.
**SERVANT GIRL:** "You were with him!" **PETER:** "I don't know him." | "You're one of them!" "I'm NOT." | "Your accent gives you away!" "I DON'T KNOW THE MAN!"
*Silence. A single crow.*
**SELAH:** And the rooster crowed. | *Across the courtyard, Jesus turns.* And Jesus turned... | and looked at Peter. | *Peter runs out into the dark.* He went outside... | and wept.
**TOBY:** *(quiet)* ...No.
*Cut: after the resurrection. Dawn by the sea. A small fire on the beach.* Weeks later, Jesus was alive. | He cooked breakfast on the beach... | on a charcoal fire.
**TOBY:** Another fire?
**SELAH:** Another fire.
**JESUS:** "Peter, do you love me?" (×3, the third softer)
**PETER (crying):** "Lord, you know everything. You know that I love you."
Three times he said no. | Three times he got to say yes.
*Present. The rooster crows again.*
**TOBY:** ...Okay. I'll be nicer to roosters.

## EP07 · THE GARDENER, *one word*
John 20:1–18; John 10:3–4 · reference shape: Sphinx ("Man.") one-word payoff

*Dawn hillside. Toby calls one name and only his sheep comes running.*
**TOBY:** Pip! ...See? She only comes when I say her name.
**SELAH:** Someone else did that once. | With one word.
*Title: MARY, the gardener*
Sunday, before sunrise. | Mary Magdalene came to the tomb... | and the stone was rolled away.
*Empty tomb, linen cloths folded.* The body was gone.
She stood outside... crying. | *Two angels in white.* "Woman, why are you crying?"
**MARY:** "They've taken my Lord... and I don't know where they've put him."
*A figure behind her, sun at his back. We never see his face clearly.* She turned around... | and saw a man. | She thought he was the gardener.
**MARY:** "Sir, if you've carried him away, tell me where... and I'll get him."
**TOBY:** *(whisper)* It's HIM, Mary...
And then he said just one word.
*Hold on silence. Gold light floods in.*
**JESUS:** "Mary."
*Title-size gold:* **MARY.**
**MARY:** "Teacher!"
**SELAH:** Jesus said: the shepherd calls his own sheep by name... | and they know his voice.
*Present. Pip nuzzles Toby.*
**TOBY:** ...Does he know MY name?
**SELAH:** Every one of them.
**TOBY:** *(to the sheep)* ...Did you hear that, Pip? *(Pip bleats)*

## Produced: GIDEON, *300 vs. the army that destroyed itself* (`episodes/ep06-gideon.*`, `videos/gideon.mp4`)
Judges 6:1-16; 7:1-22. Cold open on a broken jar; Midian swarms in like locusts; Gideon hiding in a winepress is called
"mighty warrior"; 32,000 cut to 300; the barley-loaf nightmare; jars, torches and trumpets at midnight; the enemy turns on itself.
Message: God called him what he was about to become; being small is the point. Loop: Toby's "for Gideo—" cuts to the opening smash.
Voices: Toby (Piku), Selah (Imogen), angel (Pharaoh), Gideon (Harry), Midianite soldier (Krish). Art: `art/midian.js`.
